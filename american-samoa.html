Samoan Culture & Fa’a Samoa

  • American Samoa Community College — Samoan Studies Institute Active research collections including fieldwork documentation, archival materials, and media collections on Samoan culture, oral histories, language, music, dance, and the traditional governance system of fa’a Samoa.
  • Library of Congress — American Samoa Folklife American Folklife Center collections preserving traditional arts and expressive culture from American Samoa, including 1940s South Pacific expedition recordings by Bruce and Sheridan Fahnestock.
  • Library of Congress — Margaret Mead Papers and South Pacific Ethnographic Archives Digitized correspondence, field bulletins, field data, note cards, and post-field material from Mead’s 1925–1926 fieldwork in American Samoa, part of a collection of more than 500,000 items documenting her research on child-rearing and gender roles that led to Coming of Age in Samoa.

Treaty of Cession & Naval Administration

  • National Archives — Records of the Government of American Samoa Guide to federal records documenting American Samoa’s governance, including U.S. Naval Station records (1919–1951) with information on the Fita Fita Guard Unit and naval administration.
  • Library of Congress — American Samoa Resource Guide Gateway to digital collections on American Samoa including government websites, folklife collections, and legal and genealogical resources.
  • American Samoan Digital Archives Digital collection maintained by the American Samoa Government’s Office of Archives and Records Management, featuring the 1900 Tutuila Deed of Cession, the 1904 Manu’a Deed of Cession, presidential proclamations, and public archival photographs and documents.

World War II in American Samoa

  • National Park of American Samoa — World War II National Park Service history of the 1st Samoan Battalion, Marine Corps Reserve (the “Barefoot Marines”), and the wartime fortification of Tutuila, with linked archaeological research on the island’s defensive pillboxes and gun batteries.

Archives & Reference

  • American Samoa Community College — Library Over 40,000 volumes including periodicals, databases, and audio/video materials for research on American Samoa and the Pacific.
  • Library of Congress — South Pacific Islands Web Archive Archived government documents, constitutions, legislation, census data, and development plans from Pacific island governments including American Samoa.
  • National Archives — Records of the Office of Territories Federal records documenting the administration of American Samoa and other U.S. territories.
  • National Archives at San Francisco Regional facility holding permanent federal records for American Samoa along with Hawaii, Guam, and the former Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands, including U.S. Navy administrative and court records.
  • University of Hawai‘i at Mānoa — Pacific Collection The world’s foremost collection of published materials on Polynesia, Micronesia, and Melanesia, with post-WWII government documents, historical maps, digitized photograph collections, and access to the Pacific Manuscripts Bureau’s microfilm and digital archival copies.
  • Digital Public Library of America — American Samoa Aggregated search across cultural heritage institutions contributing American Samoa materials to DPLA.

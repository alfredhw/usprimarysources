Chamorro & Carolinian Peoples

  • Library of Congress — Northern Mariana Islands Folklife American Folklife Center materials including exhibit catalogs documenting the Spanish Administration (1668–1899), German Administration (1899–1914), and Japanese Administration (1914–1944), with folk arts and cultural materials on Chamorro and Carolinian heritage.
  • Northern Marianas Humanities Council — Digital Archive Historical collections related to Northern Mariana Islands culture, history, and heritage with materials from colonial and post-WWII periods.

World War II: The Battle of Saipan & Tinian

  • National Museum of the Pacific War — Digital Archive Digitized Marine Corps photographs from Saipan and Tinian, oral histories from Pacific War veterans including Saipan residents, and official military documents from the Marianas Campaign (1944).
  • Digital Pasifik Primary source photographs and materials on WWII memorials, Suicide Cliff, Japanese military sites on Saipan, and cultural landmarks with context on Chamorro and Carolinian heritage.
  • Nuclear Museum — Tinian Island Atomic Heritage Foundation history of Tinian as headquarters of the 509th Composite Group, with photographs, oral accounts, and documents on the construction of North Field and the August 1945 atomic missions against Hiroshima and Nagasaki.
  • Guampedia — Mariana Islands Wartime Experiences through Oral Histories Community gateway to National Park Service audio and video oral histories, including accounts from Saipan, Tinian, and Rota survivors of the 1944 Marianas Campaign, with transcripts and recordings.
  • The National WWII Museum — Digital Collections: Pacific Theater of Operations Searchable database of thousands of oral history interviews and photographs from the Pacific War, including Navajo Code Talker and veteran accounts of the fighting on Saipan.

Colonial Eras & Trusteeship Period

  • National Archives — Records of the Office of Territories Federal records documenting the Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands (1947–1986) and the transition to Commonwealth status, including administrative documents and government files.
  • National Archives at San Francisco 75,000+ cubic feet of federal records (1850–1980s) including Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands administrative documents, military records, and Japanese occupation materials.
  • University of Hawai‘i at Mānoa — Trust Territory Archives Photograph Collection Digitized database of selected photographs and slides from the roughly 50,000-image Trust Territory Archives Photo Collection, illustrating the American administration of Micronesia from 1947 to 1988, including the Northern Mariana Islands.
  • Northern Marianas Humanities Council — Digital Document Collections Digital Archives Initiative collections including a compendium of German colonial literature on the Marianas (1899–1914), Spanish government records, U.S. Navy Civil Affairs files (1944–1962), Marianas Variety newspaper issues (1972–2000), and Carolinian oral histories recorded on Satawal in 1982.

Archives & Reference

  • Library of Congress — South Pacific Islands Web Archive Archived government documents, constitutions, legislation, census data, and development plans from Pacific island governments including the CNMI.
  • University of Hawai‘i at Mānoa — Pacific Collection The world’s foremost collection of published materials on Polynesia, Micronesia, and Melanesia, holding the Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands Archive of 2,169 microfilm reels documenting the American administration of the Northern Marianas through correspondence, reports, and surveys.

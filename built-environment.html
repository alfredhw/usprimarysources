Online Sources for United States History
Home › Built Environment & Historic Places

Buildings, structures, and places as primary sources: measured drawings, nomination forms, fire insurance maps, and survey records that let researchers reconstruct the American landscape building by building.

The National Documentation Programs

  • Library of Congress — HABS/HAER/HALS The flagship architectural documentation collection: measured drawings, large-format photographs, and written histories for 40,000+ historic structures, engineering works, and designed landscapes nationwide, produced since 1933 by the Historic American Buildings Survey and its engineering and landscape siblings.
  • National Park Service — National Register of Historic Places The official list of the nation’s historic places: 95,000+ listings covering 1.4 million individual resources since 1966, with searchable data, GIS downloads, and links to nomination documents—each nomination form a researched history of a place.
  • NPS — NPGallery National Register Digital Archive Searchable digital archive of National Register nomination forms, multiple-property documentation, and National Historic Landmark files, with full-text PDFs.
  • National Archives — National Register Nomination Files The permanent repository of scanned National Register nominations listed through 2012, browsable by state with full-text search across property names and descriptions.

Fire Insurance Maps

  • Library of Congress — Sanborn Fire Insurance Maps Detailed color maps of approximately 12,000 American towns and cities (1867–1970s) showing building footprints, construction materials, and street layouts—the essential source for reconstructing any American block, decade by decade.

Architectural Reference

  • SAH Archipedia Open-access, peer-reviewed encyclopedia from the Society of Architectural Historians profiling thousands of significant buildings and landscapes across all fifty states, from Indigenous architecture to the present.
  • The Cultural Landscape Foundation — What’s Out There Searchable database of North American designed landscapes with 2,800+ entries spanning two centuries, searchable by designer, type, and style.

State Historic Preservation Inventories

  • NPS — Directory of State Digitized Inventories Gateway linking to every State Historic Preservation Office’s digitized property inventory—the entry point to state-level historic building and site records nationwide.
  • Virginia Department of Historic Resources — VCRIS Statewide cultural resource database documenting 250,000+ archaeological sites and architectural properties surveyed since the 1960s, with GIS mapping and public access to survey forms. (See also Virginia.)
  • Texas Historical Commission — Historic Sites Atlas Statewide searchable atlas covering National Register properties, Recorded Texas Historic Landmarks, historical markers, cemeteries, and courthouses across all 254 counties. (See also Texas.)
  • History Colorado — Compass Database Colorado’s official cultural resource inventory: State and National Register properties, archaeological survey records, and historic building inventories across all 64 counties. (See also Colorado.)
  • Maryland Historical Trust — Medusa & MIHP Maryland’s preservation research portal: the searchable Maryland Inventory of Historic Properties and Medusa GIS database covering architectural and archaeological survey records statewide. (See also Maryland.)

Cultural Landscapes

  • NPS — Cultural Landscapes Program Cultural Landscape Inventories and Cultural Landscape Reports for historic designed and vernacular landscapes within the National Park System, with preservation guidance and reference library.

Regional Building Archives

  • Historic New England — Collections Access 125,000+ objects and 1.5 million archival items including architectural drawings, historic photographs, and building records documenting four centuries of New England life. (See also New England.)

Civic & Federal Architecture

  • General Services Administration — Center for Historic Buildings Documentation of federally owned historic buildings—courthouses, post offices, and civic architecture—with photographs and histories of the government’s built heritage.
  • NPS Maritime Heritage Program — Historic Light Stations State-by-state inventory of historic US lighthouses and light stations, with descriptions, histories, and photographs built on the 1994 national inventory.

Markers & Memorial Landscape

  • The Historical Marker Database Approximately 238,000 US roadside and commemorative markers documented with full text, GPS coordinates, and photographs—a crowd-built census of how Americans mark their history in public space.
  • NPS — National Cemeteries Documentation of the fourteen NPS-managed national cemeteries established beginning 1867, with cultural landscape and historic structure reports on these Civil War-era burial grounds. (See also Military & Wartime.)
